The launcher periodically fetches an index and a set of documents from
the community content repository and turns them into the slides shown
here. Anything it cannot understand - a malformed document, an
unreachable image, a button pointing somewhere it does not allow - is
dropped on its own, so one bad entry never blocks the rest of the feed.

This entry itself is part of that feed: it exists to explain where the
other slides come from.
