Whether you have been fragging since 1997 or just found your way back to
the Slipgate, this launcher is built for the same community that keeps
Quake II alive on custom servers, speedrun leaderboards and mod projects
around the world.

The news feed you are reading now is fed by that same community content
repository, so anyone can propose an update, a mod spotlight or a server
announcement without waiting on a launcher release.
